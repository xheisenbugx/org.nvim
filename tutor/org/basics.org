Welcome to org.nvim! This is a working copy of the tutor, a normal org
file: every key of org.nvim works here, and you learn by doing.

- Each lesson below has exercises: the headlines that start with a
  number such as =1.2=. When you have done one, a {{done}} appears at the
  end of its headline; until then it shows {{todo}}. The first line counts
  how many you have done.
- Keys are shown as they are mapped in your config. =<leader>= is your
  leader key, ={{leader}}=. =<M-x>= means Alt+x, =<S-x>= Shift+x and
  =<C-x>= Ctrl+x.
- Nothing you do here touches your notes. Make a mess if you like: =u=
  undoes, =:w= saves your progress, and =:Org tutor basics reset= starts
  the lesson over. =:Org tutor= brings you back here later.
- ={{org.help}}= lists every key of an org buffer, any time.

Move down with =j= to start.

* Lesson 1: the outline
An org file is an outline: a line that starts with stars is a headline,
and the number of stars is its level. Everything under a headline, up to
the next one of the same or a higher level, is its subtree.

** 1.1 Fold and unfold
Put the cursor on the =Kitchen= headline below and press ={{org.cycle}}=
a few times: it cycles the subtree between folded, its children and
everything. ={{org.global_cycle}}= does the same for the whole file (press
it until everything shows again).

There is nothing to check here: move on when you are done.

*** Kitchen
**** Pots
Two big ones and a small one.
**** Pans
A frying pan.

** 1.2 Insert a headline
Put the cursor on =Apples= and press ={{org.meta_return}}=: a new headline
of the same level opens, in Insert mode (above when the cursor is at the
start of the line, else below). Type =Pears= and press =<Esc>=.
={{org.insert_heading}}= does the same.

*** Fruit
**** Apples
**** Bananas

** 1.3 Promote and demote
={{org.demote_heading}}= moves a headline one level down and
={{org.promote_heading}}= one level up (={{org.demote_subtree}}= and
={{org.promote_subtree}}= take its subtree along).

Make =Carrot= a child of =Vegetables= (demote it), and =Spoon= a child of
=Drawer= (promote it).

*** Vegetables
*** Carrot
*** Drawer
***** Spoon

** 1.4 Move a subtree
={{org.meta_up}}= and ={{org.meta_down}}= move the subtree at the cursor
past its siblings. Put the days below in order: Monday, Tuesday,
Wednesday.

*** Tuesday
*** Monday
*** Wednesday

* Lesson 2: TODO items and priorities
A headline becomes a task when it starts with a TODO keyword. This file
uses =TODO= and =DONE= (the =#+TODO:= line at the top).

** 2.1 Finish a task
Put the cursor on =Buy milk= and press ={{org.todo_next}}=: TODO becomes
DONE. ={{org.shift_right}}= and ={{org.shift_left}}= cycle the keyword
too.

*** TODO Buy milk

** 2.2 Start a task
Turn =Write a letter= into a TODO item with ={{org.todo_next}}=.

*** Write a letter

** 2.3 Set a priority
Put the cursor on =Pay rent=, press ={{org.priority}}= and then =A=.
={{org.shift_up}}= and ={{org.shift_down}}= raise and lower a priority.

*** TODO Pay rent

* Lesson 3: tags
Tags go at the end of a headline, between colons: =:work:home:=.

** 3.1 Tag a headline
Put the cursor on =Prepare slides=, press ={{org.set_tags}}=, type =work=
and press =<CR>=.

*** TODO Prepare slides

* Lesson 4: dates
A headline can be scheduled (the day you plan to work on it) or have a
deadline. The date prompt takes a date like =2026-10-12=, =+3= (in three
days), =fri= or nothing (today); =<CR>= accepts it.

** 4.1 Schedule a task
Put the cursor on =Water the plants=, press ={{org.schedule}}= and accept
a date.

*** TODO Water the plants

** 4.2 Set a deadline
Put the cursor on =File the tax return=, press ={{org.deadline}}= and
accept a date. On a date, ={{org.shift_right}}= and ={{org.shift_left}}=
move it a day, and ={{org.shift_up}}= / ={{org.shift_down}}= change the
part under the cursor.

*** TODO File the tax return

* Lesson 5: lists and checkboxes
Lines starting with =-=, =+= or =1.= are list items. =[ ]= makes an item
a checkbox.

** 5.1 Add a list item
Put the cursor on =Bread= and press ={{org.meta_return}}=: a new item
opens below. Type =Butter= and press =<Esc>=.

- Milk
- Bread

** 5.2 Check a checkbox
Put the cursor on =Eggs= and press ={{org.context_action}}= (or
={{org.toggle_checkbox}}=). The =[0/2]= cookie counts the checked items.

- Shopping [0/2]
  - [ ] Eggs
  - [ ] Flour

* Lesson 6: tables
A table is lines that start with =|=. You don't have to line the columns
up yourself.

** 6.1 Align a table
Put the cursor anywhere in the table and press ={{org.context_action}}=.

| Fruit | Count |
|-
| Apple | 3 |
| Orange | 12 |

** 6.2 Add a row
Put the cursor on the =Lemon= row, press =A= for Insert mode at its end
and then ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}=: past the last field a new row
opens. Type =Kiwi=, press ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}=, type =5=, and
leave Insert mode with =<Esc>=. ={{org_insert.insert_tab}}= aligns the
table as it goes.

| Fruit | Count |
|-------+-------|
| Pear  |     7 |
| Lemon |     2 |

* Lesson 7: links
A link is written =[[target][description]]=; only the description shows.

** 7.1 Insert a link
Put the cursor at the end of the =Map= line below, press
={{org.insert_link}}=, type =*Treasure chest= as the link and =the
treasure= as the description.

Map:

*** Treasure chest
You found it.

** 7.2 Follow a link
Put the cursor on the link you made and press ={{org.open_at_point}}=: the
cursor jumps to =Treasure chest=. ={{org.store_link}}= stores a link to
where you are, for ={{org.insert_link}}= to offer later.
