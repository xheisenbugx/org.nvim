This second lesson shows how org.nvim fits into a day: catching ideas
with capture, seeing what is due in the agenda, and timing your work.
It assumes you know the keys of the first lesson (=:Org tutor basics=).

- As before, a {{done}} at the end of an exercise's headline means it is
  done, and {{todo}} that it is not yet. Keys are shown as mapped in your
  config; =<leader>= is ={{leader}}=.
- This is a working copy: =:w= saves your progress and =:Org tutor
  workflow reset= starts over. Capture and the agenda only use this
  file in the exercises, so your notes stay as they are.

* Lesson 1: capture
Capture files a note without leaving what you are doing.
={{global.capture}}= works from any buffer: it asks for a template and
files the entry where the template says (your =capture.templates=, or
=default_notes_file=). Here, to keep your notes clean, you capture into
this file with =:Org capture_here=, which files the entry at the cursor.

** 1.1 Capture a task
Put the cursor on the =Inbox= headline below and run =:Org
capture_here=. Pick a template (=t= when you have none of your own),
type a title such as =Call Bob=, then press ={{capture.finalize}}= to
file it (={{capture.kill}}= cancels).

*** Inbox

* Lesson 2: the agenda
The agenda collects the TODO items and dates of your agenda files
(=agenda_files=). ={{global.agenda}}= opens its menu. Press =<= in the
menu to restrict it to the current file, then the key of a view: =a= for
the week, =t= for every TODO item.

** 2.1 Mark a task done from the agenda
In this file, press ={{global.agenda}}=, then =<= and =t=. The agenda
lists the TODO items of this file. Move to =Call the dentist= and press
={{agenda.todo}}=: it becomes DONE, here as well. ={{agenda.quit}}=
closes the agenda.

** 2.2 See a scheduled task in the week
Schedule =Water the plants= for today with ={{org.schedule}}= (press
=<CR>= at the date prompt). Then open the week agenda for this file:
={{global.agenda}}=, =<=, =a=. In the agenda, ={{agenda.later}}= and
={{agenda.earlier}}= go a week forward and back, ={{agenda.goto}}= jumps
to the entry.

* Lesson 3: clocking
The clock records the time you spend on a task in a =CLOCK:= line under
it. Only one task is clocked at a time: clocking in here clocks out any
other task you clocked in.

** 3.1 Clock in and out
Put the cursor on =Write the report= and press ={{org.clock_in}}=. Wait a
moment, then press ={{org.clock_out}}= (it works from any buffer, and
={{global.clock_goto}}= jumps back to the clocked task). A =:LOGBOOK:=
drawer with a =CLOCK:= line appears under the headline.

** 3.2 Report the time
Put the cursor on the =Time spent= headline and press
={{org.clock_report}}=: a clock table that sums the clocked time of this
file appears below it. ={{org.context_action}}= on its =#+BEGIN:= line
updates it.
